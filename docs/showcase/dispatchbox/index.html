The brief the agent was handed

What it was asked for.

Audience

Writers with 500 to 50,000 subscribers thinking about leaving their current platform

The pitch

Dispatchbox is a newsletter platform that charges a flat fee, never emails your list on its own behalf, and lets you leave with everything in one file. The page is an editorial index of promises, each with the mechanism that makes it enforceable.

Sections it decided on

  1. Six promises as an indexed list
  2. The export file, described byte by byte
  3. No recommendation network, no cross-promotion
  4. Deliverability: our IP policy and your domain
  5. Paid subscriptions and the actual take rate
  6. Migration from the three big platforms
  7. What we are bad at
  8. Move your list
