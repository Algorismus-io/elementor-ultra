The pitch

Sinkstone is a public registry for enhanced-weathering carbon removal where every tonne is tied to soil sample data on-chain and can be audited by anyone. The page is a numbered manifesto: eleven claims, each one falsifiable.

Sections it decided on

  1. Eleven numbered claims, each with a link to the data
  2. How a tonne is measured, from field to certificate
  3. The retirement ledger, publicly readable
  4. What we refuse to list and why
  5. Third-party verifiers and their conflicts of interest
  6. Price, spread and where the money goes
  7. Failure cases we have published
  8. Open the registry
