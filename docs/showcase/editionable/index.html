Audience

The pitch

Editionable sells strictly limited editions from living artists, with the edition size, the printer and the artist's cut published on every work. The page is an asymmetric diagonal collage of works at different scales, like a well-hung wall.

Sections it decided on

  1. Diagonal hang of nine works at mixed scale
  2. Every edition, with size and artist share
  3. How an edition works, for people who have never bought one
  4. Framing, hanging and what a print actually looks like at home
  5. The artists, and how we choose them
  6. Returns, condition and resale
  7. Payment in instalments without interest
  8. Browse the current drop
