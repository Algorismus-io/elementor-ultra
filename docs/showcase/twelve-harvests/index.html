The pitch

Twelve Harvests ships one single-origin coffee a month, following the harvest calendar around the world, with the farmgate price printed on every bag. The page is a timeline spine running through the coffee year.

Sections it decided on

  1. Harvest calendar spine, January to December
  2. This month's coffee with tasting notes and farmgate price
  3. What we pay and what everyone pays, side by side
  4. Roast profile and how fresh it arrives
  5. Brew guides for three methods
  6. Subscription options and pausing
  7. Where the last 24 coffees came from
  8. Start with next month's harvest
