The pitch

Wren & Ash is a two-person furniture workshop making commissioned pieces in solid native hardwood, with a waiting list. The page is a quiet catalogue of past commissions with wood, dimensions and year, and a short note on how a commission works.

Sections it decided on

  1. Workshop name and one sentence about the wood
  2. Commission catalogue: 24 past pieces with details
  3. Where the timber comes from, tree by tree
  4. How a commission works, four stages
  5. Joinery detail plates
  6. Prices, honestly banded
  7. The wait, and why it is that long
  8. Start a conversation
