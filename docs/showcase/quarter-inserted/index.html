Audience

The pitch

Quarter Inserted is a bar with 44 restored arcade cabinets, all on free play, and a beer list that is deliberately short. The page is loud full-bleed colour blocks with hard seams, one per thing you would actually ask about.

Sections it decided on

  1. Neon block: name, free play, opening hours
  2. Cabinet list, all 44, by decade
  3. Drinks: eight taps, no cocktails, why
  4. Tournament nights and the current leaderboard rules
  5. Private hire for the whole floor
  6. Food: one pizza oven, six pizzas
  7. House rules and the over-21 after 8pm policy
  8. Find us and walk in
