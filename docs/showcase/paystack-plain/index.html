The brief the agent was handed

What it was asked for.

Audience

Owners of 5 to 50 person businesses doing payroll themselves

The pitch

Plainroll runs payroll for small UK businesses at a flat monthly fee with pension auto-enrolment and HMRC filing included. The page is a two-column ledger comparing the real total cost against the incumbents, line by line.

Sections it decided on

  1. Cost ledger: us versus the two you are considering
  2. What flat means, including the things they charge extra for
  3. Payroll day: the eleven minutes, described
  4. Pension, RTI and year-end handled
  5. Switching mid-year without breaking your figures
  6. What happens when HMRC writes to you
  7. Accountant access, free forever
  8. Run your first payroll free
