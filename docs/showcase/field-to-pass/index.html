The pitch

Field to Pass connects independent restaurants directly with growers within 90 minutes, with orders in by 9pm and delivery by 6am. The page is a horizontal filmstrip of what is available this week, because chefs shop by what is good right now.

Sections it decided on

  1. This week's filmstrip: fourteen things at their best
  2. Cut-off and delivery window, stated once
  3. Growers on the platform, with distance in km
  4. Pricing: what the grower sets, what we add
  5. Ordering: web, WhatsApp or a phone call
  6. Substitutions, shortages and what we do about them
  7. Credit terms for restaurants
  8. Open a kitchen account
