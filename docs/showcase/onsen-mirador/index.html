The pitch

Onsen Mirador is a geothermal bathhouse with a strict silent-bathing policy, sold in three-hour sessions with no treatments and no phones. The page is a split-screen scroller pairing each pool with the exact temperature and what it is for.

Sections it decided on

  1. Split hero: steam over water and the silence rule
  2. The six pools, each with temperature and purpose
  3. The circuit: how to move through it in three hours
  4. Silence, nudity and phone policy, stated plainly
  5. What we do not have: no treatments, no bar, no music
  6. Session times and how few people are in at once
  7. Accessibility, and what we can adapt
  8. Reserve a session
