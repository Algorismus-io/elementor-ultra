The pitch

Halcyon Barn is a restored 17th century threshing barn with accommodation for 22 and no in-house catering, sold as a dry-hire venue for a whole weekend. The page walks the day as a timeline, hour by hour, from Friday setup to Sunday breakfast.

Sections it decided on

  1. Weekend timeline: Friday 2pm to Sunday 11am
  2. The barn: dimensions, capacity, and what it does in the rain
  3. What is included and what genuinely is not
  4. Caterers: our list and bringing your own
  5. Sleeping 22 on site and where everyone else goes
  6. Price by season with what a Friday saves you
  7. Six couples, six very different weddings
  8. Book a viewing
