The pitch

Voltmoor makes analogue Eurorack modules with published schematics and a calibration procedure you can do yourself. The page is a catalogue grid of the module range with HP width, current draw and price visible on every tile.

Sections it decided on

  1. Module catalogue grid with HP, mA and price
  2. Signal path: what is analogue and what is not
  3. Open schematics and the repair policy
  4. Calibration you do yourself in eleven minutes
  5. Demo patches, six of them, described
  6. Power budget calculator for your case
  7. Kits versus assembled, and which to buy
  8. Order or find a dealer
