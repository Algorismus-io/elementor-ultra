The pitch

Spoke & Van is a mobile bike workshop that comes to your street or office car park and fixes the bike while you work. The page is map-first: coverage zones, next available slot per zone, and a price list you can read on a phone in ten seconds.

Sections it decided on

  1. Coverage map with today's remaining slots by zone
  2. Price list, flat and short
  3. What we carry on the van
  4. What we cannot do kerbside
  5. Booking in three taps
  6. Fleet and office contracts
  7. Same-day rescue for a puncture
  8. Book a slot
