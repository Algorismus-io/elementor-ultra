The brief the agent was handed

What it was asked for.

Audience

Parents of 7 to 12 year olds looking for after-school classes

The pitch

Byte Cubs runs small after-school coding clubs in real rooms, where kids build one finished game a term and take the code home. The page is built from big overlapping cards, like a stack of things the kids actually made.

Sections it decided on

  1. Card stack hero: three games made by kids
  2. What happens in a term, week by week
  3. Age bands and what each one builds
  4. Meet the club leaders
  5. Term dates, times and venues
  6. What parents ask before they book
  7. Trial session offer
  8. Booking with sibling discount
