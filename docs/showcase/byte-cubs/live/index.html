What happens in a term

Weeks 1 to 3 they draw it. Weeks 4 to 9 they build it and it breaks constantly. Week 10 they play each other's. Week 12 the grown-ups come and get beaten at it.

Weeks 1 to 3

They draw it

Paper first, no screens. Characters, levels, one rule the game is about. The sketch goes on the wall and stays there.

Weeks 4 to 9

They build it

And it breaks constantly. That is the lesson. Six weeks of fixing your own mess is the whole course, secretly.

Weeks 10 and 11

They swap

Everyone plays everyone else's. Kids are brutal testers and fix more bugs in one hour than we could name in three.

Week 12

Showcase night

The grown-ups come and get beaten at it. Everyone leaves with a link, the code, and a game with their name on the title screen.

Two age bands, two very different rooms

We split by what they can hold in their head, not by school year. A kid can move up mid-term if they are ready, and nobody makes a thing of it.

CUBS · 7 TO 9

Block-based, and a platformer with a boss they design

Drag-and-drop blocks, so nothing is lost to a missing semicolon. They design the boss first — its weakness, its stupid name — and build the game backwards from it. Reading level does not hold anyone back here.

CODERS · 10 TO 12

Real JavaScript, and a two-player game that works on a phone

Typed code, real files, a real editor. Two players on one phone means they have to think about somebody else using their thing — which is most of software, learned at ten.

What parents ask before they book

These four, nearly every time, usually in this order. Anything else, ring us — a person answers.

Do they need a laptop?

No. We supply every machine, charged and set up, and they never carry anything home but the code.

Do they need to be good at maths?

No. Not once in twelve weeks does it come down to maths. It comes down to whether they will try the thing again after it breaks.

Can they join late and catch up?

Yes. We have done it fourteen times. They pair with someone for a session and by the second week you cannot tell who started when.

What if they hate it?

Then they stop and we refund the rest of the term, no conversation required. It has happened twice and both times we deserved it.
