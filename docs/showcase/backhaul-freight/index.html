Audience

Owner-operator hauliers and small fleet managers

The pitch

Backhaul matches empty return legs with loads going the same way, paying in three days instead of sixty. The page is a dense bento grid of the numbers a driver cares about: rate per mile, empty miles saved, payment speed.

Sections it decided on

  1. Bento hero: live loads, rate per mile, empty miles cut
  2. Three-day payment, and what it costs you
  3. How matching works on a return leg
  4. Rates: what the shipper pays and what we take
  5. Vetting: who is allowed to post a load
  6. Documents, PODs and the paperwork on your phone
  7. For small fleets: multiple vehicles on one account
  8. Sign up with your O-licence
