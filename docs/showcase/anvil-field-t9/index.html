The pitch

The Anvil T9 is a fully ruggedised field laptop with a hot-swap battery, sunlight-readable screen and a five-year parts guarantee. The page leads with the spec sheet itself, because the buyer is comparing rows in a table, not reading prose.

Sections it decided on

  1. Full spec table as the first screen
  2. Drop, dust and thermal test results with standards cited
  3. Hot-swap battery and field-serviceable parts
  4. Ports, radios and expansion bay options
  5. Three deployments: pipeline, forestry, disaster response
  6. Procurement: lead times, framework listings, volume pricing
  7. Five-year parts and repair commitment
  8. Request a demo unit
