The brief the agent was handed

What it was asked for.

Audience

Hikers and ski tourers planning multi-day hut-to-hut routes

The pitch

Hutbook books mountain huts across six alpine countries in one basket, checking that consecutive nights are actually walkable in a day. The page is a stack of route cards you can shuffle through, each with distance, ascent and hut availability.

Sections it decided on

  1. Route card stack: six classic traverses with live beds
  2. The walkability check, explained
  3. Huts: what a night actually involves
  4. Booking a whole traverse in one basket
  5. Weather windows and free cancellation rules
  6. Half board, dietary needs and the 6pm rule
  7. Alpine club membership discounts
  8. Search a route
