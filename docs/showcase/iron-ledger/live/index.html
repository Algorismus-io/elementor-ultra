THE ROLE

You are the quartermaster, not the general.

Generals get statues. Quartermasters get blamed. You have 11,000 mouths, 14 wagons, and a bridge that will not hold both.

No one salutes when the column reaches the next valley intact. They only count the wagons that did not. Every order you sign is a subtraction from somewhere else on the page.

LINKED CAMPAIGNS

Permanent consequences, and no reminder that you caused them.

Forty linked campaigns. Abandon a wounded company in campaign three and their village refuses you bread in campaign nineteen. The game remembers everything and never reminds you.

CAMPAIGN 03 · WHAT YOU DECIDED

The mill road is flooding. You leave forty wounded and one lamp, and the column makes the ridge before dark.

CAMPAIGN 19 · WHAT IT COST

Sixteen turns later the same valley has bread to sell and will not sell it to you. No cutscene says why. The ledger does.

04 — WHAT IT IS NOT

There is only a better and a worse retreat.

No base building. No hero units. No respawns, no difficulty slider, no way to win the war.

NOT IN THIS GAME

No base building

Nothing to construct. The camp is wherever the column stopped, and it is gone by morning.

NOT IN THIS GAME

No hero units

No one on the roster is irreplaceable, and no one is exempt from the ration table.

NOT IN THIS GAME

No respawns

A company lost in campaign six is absent from the manifest for the remaining thirty-four.

NOT IN THIS GAME

No difficulty slider

The war is fixed. Only your bookkeeping changes, and only ever by degree.

05 — FOUR FRAMES

Four frames from the second retreat.

FRAME 01 · THE ROUTE

Two roads out of the valley. One is shorter and takes the wagons; the other keeps the wounded.

FRAME 02 · CLOSING THE TURN

The turn will not close until every line balances. It will let you balance it badly.

FRAME 03 · CAMP, 03:40

Fires are a ration too. Cold nights cost boots; warm nights cost the woodpile.

FRAME 04 · THE MANIFEST

Every name on the roll, and the date it stopped moving.

Sortable, searchable, and never cleared between campaigns. It is the only monument in the game.
