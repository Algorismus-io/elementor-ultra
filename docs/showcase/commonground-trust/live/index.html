04 — WHAT THE MONEY BUYS

Twelve pounds a month, in square metres.

12 pounds a month protects roughly 40 square metres a year, and keeps protecting it after you stop.

That is the whole arithmetic. Land in the Wenn valley runs at about 6,500 an acre; a standing order becomes a deposit, a deposit becomes a contract, and the contract does not lapse when your circumstances change.

PATH B

A conservation easement

Keep your land. Give up the right to ruin it.

01

You stay the owner and you keep farming it.

02

A covenant registered against the title removes the development and drainage rights, permanently.

03

We monitor it once a year, in person, and the covenant binds every owner after you.

Four Wenn farms have done this since 2019. Two of them are still in the same families.
