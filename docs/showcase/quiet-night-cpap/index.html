The pitch

The Quiet Night A2 is a CPAP machine designed around the reasons people stop using them: noise, mask pressure and the water chamber. The page leads with a measured spec comparison against the two machines the reader already tried.

Sections it decided on

  1. Spec comparison table against two common machines
  2. Measured noise at the pillow, in decibels, with method
  3. The mask problem and the three sizes included
  4. Humidifier that does not need distilled water
  5. Data: what it records and who can see it
  6. Insurance, prescription and returns
  7. Clinical evidence and regulatory clearances
  8. Order or request a 30-night trial
