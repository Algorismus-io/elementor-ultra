The pitch

Kindling Health is a specialist menopause clinic run by video, with menopause-trained doctors, prescriptions delivered, and unlimited follow-up for a flat annual fee. The page reads as one long, calm, honest letter rather than a product pitch.

  4. Prescribing: what we can and cannot offer
  5. The doctors, with their menopause qualifications
  6. Cost, and what happens if you stop paying
  7. Three patient accounts, in their own words
  8. Book a first appointment
