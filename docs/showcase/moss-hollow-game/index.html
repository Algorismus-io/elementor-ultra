Audience

The pitch

Moss Hollow is a slow farming and foraging game you play for ten minutes a day, sold once for a fixed price with no energy system, no ads and no purchases. The page is a soft bento grid of the things it deliberately does not do.

Sections it decided on

  1. Bento hero: no ads, no timers, no purchases, one price
  2. What you actually do in ten minutes
  3. The seasons, and what changes in each
  4. Offline, on a plane, in a tunnel
  5. Accessibility: one-hand, colourblind, no time pressure
  6. The soundtrack and the person who wrote it
  7. Price, platforms and family sharing
  8. Buy once
