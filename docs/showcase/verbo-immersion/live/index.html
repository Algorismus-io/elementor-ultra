The spine · week by week

What you can do by Friday

Not a syllabus. A list of things you could not do on Monday and can do by the end of the week, out loud, to a person.

1

Introduce yourself without translating

Week 1: you will introduce yourself and ask three follow-up questions without translating in your head. It will be exhausting.

2

Order, ask, misunderstand, ask again

Week 2: you will order food, ask for directions and misunderstand the answer. Then you will ask a second time, differently, and get it.

3

Talk about yesterday, badly

Week 3: the past tense arrives and it arrives wrong. You will tell the room what you did at the weekend and nobody will stop you mid-sentence.

4

Argue about something small

Week 4: you will argue about something small. Food, weather, football. You will be wrong grammatically and understood completely.

5

Take a phone call with no notes

Week 5: your teacher rings you without warning. Four minutes, no script, no screen to read from. Everyone survives it.

6

Explain your job to a stranger

Week 6: you will explain what you do for a living to someone who does not do it, twice, in two different ways, until the second way is shorter.

7

Choose your topic and defend it

Week 7: you choose the subject for week eight and you argue for that choice in the language. Homework is one conversation with someone outside the class.

8

Fifteen minutes with a stranger

Week 8: fifteen minutes with a stranger over video, unscripted, on a topic you chose in week seven. We record it and send it to you, and you should keep it.

The whole method

Why four students and not twelve

Four students. In a class of twelve you speak for six minutes an hour. In a class of four you speak for twenty-two. That single number is the whole method.

The one rule

No English after minute three

The first three minutes are for admin and nerves. After that the room switches and stays switched for eighty-seven minutes. You will point, mime, draw, use the wrong word on purpose and get there anyway — and the teacher will help you get there in the language, never around it.

Homework is only conversation. No worksheets, no gap-fills, no streaks to protect.

Before any money moves

A placement call before you pay

Nobody buys before a twenty-minute placement call. Roughly one in six people we speak to are told to start a level higher than they expected.

It is a conversation, not a test. Ten minutes in English about why you are doing this, ten minutes in the language you are learning — however little of it you have. We tell you the level, the next intake date and, sometimes, that you should not book yet.

Price, schedule and what happens if you miss a week

Price

£420 for the eight weeks, sixteen sessions. Paid after your placement call, in one payment or two.

Schedule

Twice a week, ninety minutes each, evenings at 18:30 or 19:30 UK time. You keep the same two slots for the whole course.

If you miss a week

Miss up to two and you can sit them with another cohort at no cost. Miss four and we move you to the next intake for free.

Class size

Four students. Never five. If a cohort fills, you go to the next intake rather than into a bigger room.

What you need

A camera, headphones and ninety minutes where nobody will interrupt you. No textbook — we send the material each Sunday.
