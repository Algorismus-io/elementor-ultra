The pitch

Saltmarrow runs river-mouth interception booms that catch plastic before it reaches open ocean, and publishes the tonnage from every device weekly. The page is one long honest column, including the parts of the mission that are not working.

Sections it decided on

  1. Live tonnage from 14 booms, this week
  2. Why river mouths and not the open ocean
  3. What we actually recover, sorted by type
  4. The three booms that are underperforming and why
  5. Where the plastic goes after we lift it
  6. Volunteer days: what a Saturday involves
  7. Funding: our costs per tonne, published
  8. Give or come out on a boat
