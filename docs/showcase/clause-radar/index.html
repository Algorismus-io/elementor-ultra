The pitch

Clause Radar reviews inbound contracts against a company's own playbook and produces a redline with the reason for each change cited to the playbook clause. The page is a specification table: every checked clause type, what it flags, and the false positive rate.

Sections it decided on

  1. Clause coverage table with detection and false positive rates
  2. How your playbook is encoded, and by whom
  3. A worked redline of a real MSA clause
  4. What it refuses to do without a lawyer
  5. Where your documents go and how long they stay
  6. Word and email integration
  7. Pricing per seat with unlimited documents
  8. Book a playbook session
