Audience

Parents of under-tens, especially ones whose child is frightened

The pitch

Little Molar is a children's dental practice built around the first visit going well, with a no-treatment first appointment and a room the child chooses. The page is warm, simple and reassuring, in the classic hero-cards-testimonial shape because parents want familiar here.

Sections it decided on

  1. Centred hero with the first-visit promise
  2. Three cards: no treatment first, choose your room, tell-show-do
  3. What happens if your child says no
  4. The team and their pediatric training
  5. Costs, NHS places and what a check-up runs
  6. Parent testimonial about a frightened child
  7. Emergency same-day slots
  8. Book a first visit
