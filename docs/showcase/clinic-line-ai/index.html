The pitch

Clinic Line answers a clinic's phone when reception cannot, books into the existing diary, and hands off to a human on anything clinical. The page is a straightforward proof-driven pitch to a busy practice manager who is not shopping for AI.

Sections it decided on

  1. Centred hero with a missed-call statistic
  2. Three cards: answers, books, escalates
  3. Listen to a real call recording
  4. Diary systems it plugs into
  5. What it will never do
  6. Practice manager testimonial with numbers
  7. Pricing and 30-day cancel
  8. Book a 15 minute setup call
