What it was asked for.

Audience

Solo freelancers who dread the annual tax return

The pitch

Ledgerless is a business account that automatically sets aside tax, files the return, and tells the user one number they can actually spend. The page is built almost entirely from that one number, set enormous.

Sections it decided on

  1. Giant safe-to-spend number as the whole first screen
  2. Three balances explained: yours, the taxman's, the buffer
  3. How the set-aside is calculated, month by month
  4. Filing day: what actually happens
  5. Fees, stated as one sentence
  6. Who it is not for
  7. Regulatory and deposit protection statement
  8. Open an account
