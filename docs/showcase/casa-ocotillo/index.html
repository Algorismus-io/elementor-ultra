The pitch

Casa Ocotillo is an eleven-room adobe hotel in the high desert with a spring-fed pool, no televisions, and one restaurant that serves whatever arrived that morning. The page is a split-screen scroller: still image on one side, quiet detail on the other.

Sections it decided on

  1. Split hero: courtyard image and arrival note
  2. Eleven rooms, three types, honest sizes
  3. The pool and the hours it is silent
  4. The kitchen: one menu, changed daily
  5. What there is to do, and what there deliberately is not
  6. Getting here: the last 14km are unpaved
  7. Rates by season with minimum stays
  8. Availability check
