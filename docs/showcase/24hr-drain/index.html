The pitch

Hardin Drains is a 24-hour emergency drainage company. The page exists to get a panicking person to call within five seconds, then answer the two questions they will ask. Deliberately brutal: enormous slabs, no imagery, no charm.

Sections it decided on

  1. Yellow slab: phone number at maximum size
  2. Black slab: price before we come out
  3. Red slab: what to do in the next two minutes
  4. White slab: what we can clear tonight
  5. Black slab: response times by area
  6. Yellow slab: how we take payment
  7. Grey slab: landlord and commercial accounts
  8. Phone number again
