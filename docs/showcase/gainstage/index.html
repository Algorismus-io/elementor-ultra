The pitch

Gainstage edits a podcast by editing its transcript: cut a sentence, the audio goes with it, and the room tone stays smooth. The page is a bento grid where every tile is one thing the tool removes from a Sunday afternoon.

Sections it decided on

  1. Bento hero: transcript tile, waveform tile, timer tile
  2. Cut the words, keep the room tone
  3. Filler word sweep with a before and after
  4. Multitrack sync for remote guests
  5. Chapters, show notes and clips generated from the edit
  6. Export targets and what each one does
  7. Pricing by hours of audio, not seats
  8. Start a free episode
