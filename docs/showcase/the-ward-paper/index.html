Audience

The pitch

The Ward is a reader-owned local newspaper covering one city's council, courts and planning, with four reporters and no proprietor. The page is a long single column in the voice of an editor writing to neighbours, with the membership ask at the end.

Sections it decided on

  1. Editor's letter, no images above the fold
  2. What we cover every single week, without fail
  3. The four reporters and their beats
  4. What happened when nobody was in the council chamber
  5. Co-op ownership: one member one vote
  6. Print, email and the noticeboard
  7. Membership and the free tier that stays free
  8. Join the co-op
