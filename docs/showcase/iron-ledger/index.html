The pitch

Iron Ledger is a turn-based tactics game where you play the quartermaster of a losing army and every decision is about supply, not combat. The page is aggressive colour blocks with hard seams, each one a different mechanic, ending in a wishlist button.

Sections it decided on

  1. Black block: title, one line, wishlist
  2. Rust block: you are the quartermaster, not the general
  3. Brass block: the ledger system explained with a real turn
  4. Moss block: 40 campaigns, permanent consequences
  5. Bone block: what it is not (no base building, no heroes)
  6. Screenshot band, four frames
  7. Dev log and early access dates
  8. Wishlist on every store
