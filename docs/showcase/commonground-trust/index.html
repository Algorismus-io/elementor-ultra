The brief the agent was handed

What it was asked for.

Audience

Regular donors and landowners in one river watershed

The pitch

Common Ground Trust buys and permanently protects floodplain and grassland along a single river, parcel by parcel. The page is a map first: every acre already protected, every parcel currently under offer, and what a donation buys in square metres.

Sections it decided on

  1. Watershed map with protected parcels shaded
  2. Counter: acres held, acres under offer, acres lost this year
  3. Parcel dossier: the three we are trying to buy now
  4. What your money buys, in square metres
  5. Species that came back, with dates
  6. Leave land, not just money: bequest and easement paths
  7. Accounts: where every pound went last year
  8. Standing order sign-up
