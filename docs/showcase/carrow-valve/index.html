The pitch

Carrow has forged industrial ball and gate valves in the same works since 1911 and sells to refineries, water authorities and shipbuilders. The page is a selection table as the hero, because engineers arrive knowing their pressure class and bore.

Sections it decided on

  1. Valve selection table: size, class, material, seat, lead time
  2. Standards and approvals held, with certificate numbers
  3. Materials: what we forge and what we will not cast
  4. Testing regime: hydrostatic, seat, cryogenic
  5. Sectors served with one reference job each
  6. Spares and 40-year part availability
  7. Drawing downloads and CAD models
  8. Request a quotation
