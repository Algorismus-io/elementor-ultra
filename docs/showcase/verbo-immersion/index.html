The brief the agent was handed

What it was asked for.

Audience

Adults who did three years of an app and still cannot hold a conversation

The pitch

Verbo runs eight-week live immersion courses with four students per class and homework that is only conversation. The page is a timeline spine of the eight weeks, showing exactly what a student can do at the end of each one.

Sections it decided on

  1. Week-by-week spine: what you can do by Friday
  2. Why four students and not twelve
  3. The teachers and where they are from
  4. Languages and levels offered
  5. The one rule: no English after minute three
  6. Placement call before you pay
  7. Price, schedule and what happens if you miss a week
  8. Book a placement call
