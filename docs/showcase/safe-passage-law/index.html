The pitch

Firstlight Legal provides free legal representation to unaccompanied minors in asylum appeals, and trains pro bono solicitors to take cases. The page is a single sober column: the situation, the numbers, the ask, and a clearly separated route for someone who needs help now.

Sections it decided on

  1. Help-now bar for anyone who needs a lawyer today
  2. The situation in four paragraphs and three numbers
  3. What representation changes, with outcome data
  4. One case, told with permission
  5. For lawyers: the pro bono training route
  6. Where donations go, line by line
  7. Governance, funding sources and our red lines
  8. Give monthly
