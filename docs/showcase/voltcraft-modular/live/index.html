02 — SIGNAL PATH

What is analogue, and what is not

The audio path is entirely analogue. The only digital parts are the tuning helper and the preset memory, and both can be bypassed with a jumper on the back.

We say this plainly because the word analogue gets stretched. No DSP sits between your input jack and your output jack on any module in the range — measure it if you like, the test points are marked on the board.

ANALOGUE

Oscillator cores

Discrete, matched pair, temperature compensated

ANALOGUE

Filters and VCAs

Transistor ladder and OTA, no lookup tables anywhere

ANALOGUE

Envelopes and LFOs

RC timing, voltage controlled, sub-millisecond attack

DIGITAL

Tuning helper

A microcontroller reading pitch. Jumper JP1 removes it.

DIGITAL

Preset memory

Stores knob positions only. Jumper JP2 removes it.

04 — CALIBRATION

Eleven minutes, at your desk

Eleven minutes with a multimeter and a tuner, four trimmers, and a printed card that walks you through it. No proprietary jig, no returning it to the factory.

00:00

Warm up

Power the module in the case and leave it for three minutes. Cold trimming is why people think analogue drifts.

03:00

Scale, TR1

Play C2 and C4, adjust until the octave is exact on the tuner. Two passes, it converges.

07:00

Offset, TR2

Set the low reference against the meter at the marked test point. One turn, rarely more.

09:00

Shape, TR3 and TR4

Trim the triangle apex and the pulse symmetry by ear or by scope. Write the date on the card.

05 — DEMO PATCHES

Six patches, written out in full

Not videos. Descriptions you can follow with cables in your hand, using modules you can see on the tiles above.

01

Through-zero drone

VX-3 · VX-5 · VX-10

Both VCO cores at 55Hz, one modulating the other through zero, into the state variable filter in notch and out to the spring tank at full dwell. Sits still for twenty minutes and never repeats.

02

Ladder kick

VX-4 · VX-6 · VX-8

Self-oscillate the ladder, pitch it with a fast decay envelope, and shape the tail on the exponential VCA. Tune the resonance trimmer for the body you want.

03

Clocked sample chatter

VX-2 · VX-11 · VX-3

Sample and hold on white noise, clocked from the sequencer gate bus, into the second VCO exponential input. Attenuvert it down until it is pitch and not noise.

04

Two-hand generative

VX-7 · VX-1 · VX-5

Two function generators cycling at unrelated rates, cross-patched through the attenuverter to each other, sweeping the filter cutoff from both directions at once.

05

BBD feedback bed

VX-9 · VX-8 · VX-12

Delay output back into its own input past unity, VCA in the loop to keep it from running away, cue bus so you can hear it building before it hits the mix.

06

West coast bass

VX-3 · VX-7 · VX-8

Single VCO into the function generator used as a lowpass gate substitute, struck by the sequencer gates. Short strikes, long strikes, one knob between them.
