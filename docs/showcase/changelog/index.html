Changelog

What this is: a public record of every change we make to the exjsx demo archive after publication — every business renamed, every invented identifier replaced, every page taken down, and every complaint actioned.

Why it exists: the archive is fifty fictional businesses generated by AI, and the names and details were not checked against the real world before they were built. Some of them collided with real businesses and real identifiers. We would rather show our working than quietly edit and hope nobody noticed.

If something here concerns you, or something is missing, email legal@wpos.ai.

Scheduled before launch

These are corrections identified by our own pre-launch review. They are not done yet. They are listed here now so that the list exists before anyone has to ask for it, and each will move to the log below, with a date, when it ships.

The log

14 August 2026 — legal documents revised

The publisher of this archive is Algorismus LLC, a Texas company. An earlier draft of these notices was written on the assumption that the publisher was based in the United Kingdom, and led with UK and EU law as a result. That was wrong.

The Terms of Use, this changelog, the content notice, the AI content statement and the privacy notice have been rewritten to state United States and Texas law where it governs, and to keep UK and EU law where it still reaches visitors from those places. The governing law and the venue in the Terms changed. Our takedown commitment did not change, and neither did anything we tell you about the content: it is all still fictional and it is all still AI-generated.

14 August 2026 — this archive published

Fifty fictional business websites, generated and built by AI agents in a single working day, published at /showcase/ with a permanent content notice on every page.
