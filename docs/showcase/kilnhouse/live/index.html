Four glazes

Four glazes: Ash, Ox Blood, Fig, Raw. Every one is food-safe and lead-free, tested twice a year. Each pot takes the fire differently, so no two leave the kiln identical.

Ash

Pale, matte, moves in the fire

Ox Blood

Deep red, pooling at the foot

Fig

Bruised purple, near black in shade

Raw

Unglazed outside, glazed within

How it is fired

Four people. Two days in the kiln. One address.

01

Thrown

Every piece is thrown on the wheel in Coimbra, one of six shapes, no moulds.

02

Glazed

Dipped by hand in one of four glazes, food-safe and lead-free, tested twice a year.

03

Fired

Flame-fired to 1280°C over two days. The kiln decides the last ten percent.

04

Checked and boxed

Rung for tone, checked for crazing, signed by the four people who made it.

What it takes, and what it will not

Dishwasher yes. Thermal shock no. Do not take it from freezer to a hot oven and it will see out your kitchen.

IT CAN TAKE

+

The dishwasher, every day, forever

+

The oven to 260°C, lid on or off

+

Gas, electric, induction, open flame

+

Metal spoons, knives, whisks

+

Straight from oven to table to fridge

IT CANNOT

—

Freezer to a hot oven — thermal shock

—

A cold sink under a hot pan

—

Empty on a high flame for ten minutes

—

Bleach or oven cleaner, it does not need it

—

Seasoning. There is no coating to feed.
