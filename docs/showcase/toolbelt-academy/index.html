Audience

18 to 26 year olds deciding against university, and their parents

The pitch

Toolbelt matches young people with paid apprenticeships in electrical, plumbing, HVAC and welding, and shows the actual earnings curve versus a degree. The page is a dossier with a sticky sidebar: trades on the left, hard numbers on the right.

Sections it decided on

  1. Trade selector sidebar and earnings headline
  2. Earnings curve: apprentice versus graduate, year by year
  3. Four trades, with what the work is actually like
  4. What an apprenticeship costs you, which is nothing
  5. Entry requirements, honestly stated
  6. Employers hiring right now, by region
  7. For parents: the questions you are about to ask
  8. Apply in six minutes
