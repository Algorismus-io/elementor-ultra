Audience

Individuals with five years of messy on-chain history and a filing deadline

The pitch

Basis reconstructs cost basis across chains, exchanges and DeFi positions and produces a filing-ready report with an audit trail for every number. The page is a numbered manifesto of the nine ways crypto tax software normally lies to you, and what Basis does instead.

Sections it decided on

  1. Nine numbered failures of every other tool
  2. How we handle the ones nobody handles: LP, wrapping, bridges
  3. Missing data: what we do instead of guessing
  4. The audit trail behind every single line
  5. Jurisdictions and methods supported
  6. Your keys, your data, and what we can see
  7. Pricing by transaction count
  8. Import your first wallet
